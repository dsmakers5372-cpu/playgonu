Sources and references

PlayGonu's descriptions of each game's history, names and rules draw on the published sources below. Gonu rules vary by region and by family, so a source may describe a variant slightly differently from the version playable here.

The playable rules are PlayGonu's own implementation. The AI statistics quoted in our strategy guide come from games our own AIs played against each other, not from a published study.
