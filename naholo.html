How Naholo-gonu works Solo puzzle
  • This one's for one player — no opponent, no AI. "나홀로" means "all alone."
  • 32 pegs fill a 33-point cross-shaped board, leaving only the center empty.
  • Jump a peg over an adjacent peg into the empty point just beyond it, in a straight line — this removes the jumped peg.
  • Keep jumping until no more jumps are possible. The goal: clear down to as few pegs as you can — 1 is a perfect clear.
Same idea as Western peg solitaire

Naholo-gonu is a real, documented Korean traditional game that plays essentially identically to the classic peg-solitaire puzzle — same board, same jump-and-remove mechanic, same goal.
