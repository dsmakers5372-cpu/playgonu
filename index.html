About PlayGonu

PlayGonu is where traditional Korean Gonu games get a modern upgrade for the web. We collect rules that differ from region to region, with their sources, and we use many games against our AI to find the points where a match drags on, then add rules that suit the web and publish them. The rule that a full board during placement is a draw, and the 20-move limit in the movement phase, were set that way.
