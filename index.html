How Jul-gonu works
  • Each player starts with 4 pieces lined up on their own edge of the board.
  • Move one piece per turn, one step along a line.
  • Trap an enemy piece between two of yours in a straight line to capture it.
  • Win by reducing your opponent to one piece, or leaving them with no legal move.
What is Gonu?

A family of Korean folk board games, centuries old — this is Jul-gonu, the simplest. Pick another variant above anytime.
