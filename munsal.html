How Munsal-gonu works Low-confidence rules
  • Each player starts with 5 pieces lined up on their own edge of a 5x5-point board.
  • Move one piece per turn, one step along a line — or jump over an adjacent piece (yours or an opponent's) onto the empty point directly beyond it.
  • Jumping over an opponent's piece captures it. Jumping your own piece just hops over it.
  • If jumping opens up another jump for the same piece, you must keep jumping with it before your turn ends.
  • Win by capturing every enemy piece, or leaving your opponent with no legal move.
A source note

Munsal-gonu's name comes from 문살, the lattice pattern of a traditional Korean door. Documented sources for its exact rules are scarce — this is our best reconstruction from what's available, same as Hobak-gonu.
